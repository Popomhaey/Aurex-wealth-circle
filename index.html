Important risk disclosure

Forex and CFD trading is speculative and involves significant risk of loss. Automated execution can increase consistency of execution, but it cannot eliminate market risk, drawdown, slippage, spread costs, gaps, liquidity risk, broker risk or technical failure.

  • Past performance does not guarantee future results.
  • No monthly return, profit, win rate or drawdown level is guaranteed.
  • Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Do not increase leverage or copy exposure solely because historical results were positive.
  • Historical drawdown is not a promise of the maximum future drawdown.
  • Performance can differ between the master/track-record account and a client's account because of execution, spread, slippage, timing, leverage, account size and settings.
  • AUREX does not represent that use of an EA or copy strategy is suitable for every person.

Frequently asked

Clear answers before you decide.

Do I send my trading money to AUREX?

No. Under the client-account setup and strategy-copy structures described here, trading capital is held in the client's own broker account. AUREX service fees are separate from trading capital.

Does the bot guarantee profit?

No. There is no guaranteed return. The system automates a programmed trading strategy; market conditions can produce profits or losses.

Can I see the trades?

Yes. With the own-account setup, trades are visible through the client's trading platform. With supported copy trading, copied positions appear in the client's broker/platform account subject to the platform's execution and settings.

Do I need to be a Forex expert?

No, but you should understand the basic risks of leveraged forex/CFD trading. AUREX can provide education and technical guidance, but automation does not replace your responsibility to understand the account you are using.

What is the Myfxbook page?

It is a third-party tracking page used to display historical account statistics. It is evidence of a particular account's recorded history, not a promise that another account will achieve the same result.

Can I stop the bot or copying?

Yes, subject to the broker/platform's controls and any applicable open-position considerations. Clients remain responsible for monitoring their account and deciding whether to continue using the system.

What fees do I pay?

For the AUREX Quant Bot setup shown on this page: ₦200,000 one-time setup and bot access, plus ₦15,000 monthly VPS maintenance and technical support. Strategy-copy costs and any performance-sharing arrangements are determined by the relevant platform's current terms.
